My Story

Crafting for the Web with Passion & Purpose

Hello! I'm BANSH. My journey started at the intersection of visual art and computer science. What fascinated me from day one was how thoughtful code combined with good typography could solve tangible problems for businesses and people.

Over the last 2+ years, I have worked with entrepreneurs, startups, and creative agencies worldwide. Whether crafting a tailored WordPress website that empowers business owners to manage content effortlessly, or building a bespoke frontend web experience using pure semantic HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, my goal is always the same: delivering digital products that are fast, intuitive, and visually memorable.

When I'm not writing clean markup or fine-tuning vectors in Figma and Illustrator, I'm exploring modern design trends, optimizing performance benchmarks, and mentoring fellow developers.
